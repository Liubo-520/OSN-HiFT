# Source attribution and reuse notices

Checked 23 October 2026. This computational package is shared for noncommercial
research and reproducibility. It does not claim a single license for all files.

## Original database and snapshots

The Open Membrane Database (OMD), its contributors and original source-paper
authors supplied the underlying membrane measurements:
https://openmembranedatabase.org/ . The OMD about page links the
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license:
https://openmembranedatabase.org/about-the-open-membrane-database/ and
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . Retain attribution and the
noncommercial notice for OMD-derived material, including the later local export.

The unchanged 8 April 2025 snapshot was obtained from
https://github.com/BAMresearch/onf-database . Its README declares CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), although the LICENSE file it
mentions was absent from the repository root at this check. That declaration is
preserved here alongside the upstream OMD notice; it is not treated as permission
to expand rights in the underlying database. The historical download manifest
records the mirror's declaration and is not rewritten.

Cite the OMD4SRNF database paper, S. Van Buggenhout et al.,
"Open and FAIR Data for Nanofiltration in Organic Media: A Unified Approach",
Journal of Membrane Science 713 (2025), 123356,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.memsci.2024.123356 . The 2025 mirror accompanies
S. Glass, P. Merten, T. Brinkmann and V. Filiz, "Interpretable machine learning
for predicting membrane performance of organic solvent nanofiltration membranes
using an open-access database", Journal of Membrane Science,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.memsci.2026.126088 . Exact bibliography metadata and
per-row source-paper references are retained in the verification records/data.

Changes made here: canonicalized duplicate paper-reference strings, selected
task-specific domains, verified solvent descriptors, added derived features,
splits, predictions and fitted model artifacts. The raw snapshots are unchanged.
The later export was already local when the audit began; its hash is recorded,
but an acquisition log is unavailable. The label date is not proof of prospective
blinding. Public access does not imply database-creator endorsement.
